AMANO PROPS - STYLE GUIDE (Crystal Worlds v1.0.2)
=========================================

1. SCALE: 1 unit = 1 meter (alien tree 8 m, energy crystal 1-8 m, boulder 1-3 m, ground patch 10 x 10 m).
2. ORIGIN: on the ground, centre of the footprint (ground patches: centre of the patch, surface near z = 0).
3. PALETTE: ONE texture Textures/props_palette.png, 8 columns x 10 rows of 16 px cells. Every face is
   UV-mapped to the center of one palette cell. Cells are only appended, never moved. Palettes dusk / mist / night keep the layout.
4. STYLE: low poly, large smooth facets, slim silhouettes, soft light. No characters, no brands, no text.
5. NAMING: Category folder + Asset_Name, e.g. Trees/Tree_Slim_A, Alien/Energy_Crystal_Spire.
