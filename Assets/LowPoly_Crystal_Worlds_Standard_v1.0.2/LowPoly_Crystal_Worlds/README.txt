Low Poly Crystal Worlds v1.0.2 - Standard edition
================================================

FREE (name your own price, from $0): 22 hand-picked pieces from both worlds (alien planet and crystal night), GLB / FBX / OBJ, 4 palettes (day, dusk, mist, night).

Two low poly worlds in a soft, faceted style: a crystal and ice night (glowing blue crystals, frozen arches, glow mushrooms,
snowy pines) and an alien planet (purple energy crystals as an extraterrestrial power source, glowing alien plants, floating rocks,
dome hut, landing pad, energy core). Ground patches of 10 x 10 m tile side by side, and diorama bases for showcase scenes.
No characters. Part of the "Amano Props" series (1 unit = 1 meter, shared 2 m grid).

CONTENTS
  Models/GLB/        glTF binary, day colors (Godot 4 and Blender tested; usable in Unity with glTFast, Unreal 5, Three.js)
  Models/FBX/        FBX (Unity, Unreal, Blender) - the palette texture sits next to the models and loads automatically
  Models/OBJ/        OBJ + MTL
  Textures/          props_palette.png + props_emission.png (glow map)
                     + 3 alternative palettes: _dusk / _mist / _night.png
  Docs/              STYLE_GUIDE.txt, Palette_Chart.png

SPECS
  - Scale: 1 unit = 1 meter. Origin on the ground at the centre of each piece. Ground_* patches are 10 x 10 m with a
    flat border at height 0, so they join with each other without a gap (rotate in 90 degree steps if you like).
  - One shared material / one 128 x 160 palette texture for the whole pack. Use NEAREST (point) filtering.
    Alternative palettes: Textures/props_palette_dusk.png, _mist.png, _night.png (same UVs: swap the image).
    The Complete and Pro editions also have ready-made GLB sets for each palette (Models/GLB_Dusk, GLB_Mist, GLB_Night).
    The glowing crystals, fire and moon keep their color in every palette.
  - Glow (energy crystals, glowing plants, moon, fire) uses the emission map Textures/props_emission.png (optional).
  - Models are Z-up in Blender and exported Y-up (glTF / FBX / OBJ).
  - Faces: 27 to 358 per object (most pieces are 40-220).
  - Engines: Godot and Blender tested; GLB/FBX/OBJ also usable in Unity and Unreal.
